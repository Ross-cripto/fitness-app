# Privacy

Momentum works fully offline.

- **No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no network requests** made by the app for its own use.
- **Where your data lives:** in one JSON file in the app's private Documents folder on your device (profile, workouts, body weights). Deleting the app deletes it. *Settings → Delete all data* erases it too.
- **Apple Health is optional.** If you turn it on, the app saves finished workouts and body weight to Health and reads steps and active energy. That data stays in Health on your device; you can revoke access at any time in *Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices*.
- **YouTube links** open in your browser only when you tap them; they are searches for the exercise name and share nothing else.
- **Backups** (when you export your data) are files you choose where to save.

Because the code is open, you can check all of this in the source.
