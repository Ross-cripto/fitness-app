# Code of Conduct

We want Momentum to be a welcoming project for everyone who wants to help people move more, whatever their experience, body, background or language.

**Expected:** be respectful and patient, give and accept feedback graciously, assume good faith, and focus on what is best for the people who use the app.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discrimination, shaming of bodies or fitness levels, personal attacks, publishing others' private information, or other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break these rules, and may block people who repeatedly do so. To report a problem, open an issue marked "conduct" or contact the maintainers through the email on their GitHub profiles.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1 (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
